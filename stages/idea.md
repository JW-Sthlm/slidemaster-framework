# Idea

The idea arrives as a voice prompt. Talk about it out loud and unedited, by dictation or as a typed stream of thought. Talk about the idea, not the whole talk: chapters, slides and a run-through come later. In the same take, say who the audience is, what kind of event it is, how long the slot is and which language you will speak.

The assistant saves the transcript below exactly as it came out. It never fixes your grammar, order or wording, and it never rewrites your idea. Later rounds get their own dated sections.
